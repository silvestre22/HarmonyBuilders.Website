About Harmony Builders

One partner, from groundbreaking to handover and beyond.

Harmony Builders Sdn Bhd is a Grade G7 construction company, the highest classification awarded by Malaysia's Construction Industry Development Board (CIDB). That grade allows us to undertake projects of unlimited value, and we bring the same discipline to a family bungalow as to a factory.

What makes us different is what happens after the build. We are also an asset management company: we refurbish ageing bungalows and semi-detached homes, then manage, rent and sell them. Add our in-house 3D layout and interior design studio, and you have one team that can imagine, build, revive and look after your property.

  • Design and build, all under one roof
  • Residential, commercial and industrial expertise
  • Transparent costing and milestone reporting
  • Long-term property care after completion

The Harmony Revive Programme

We turn tired homes into thriving assets

Our signature model connects construction and asset management. We give older bungalows and semi-detached homes a second life, then keep them working for their owners.

  1. 01

    Assess & Acquire

    We identify ageing landed homes with strong potential and carry out a thorough structural and market assessment.

  2. 02

    Design & Refurbish

    Our designers plan a modern layout in 3D, and our G7 team rebuilds, upgrades and finishes it to a high standard.

  3. 03

    Manage & Maintain

    We handle tenants, maintenance and inspections, so the property stays in excellent condition year after year.

  4. 04

    Rent or Sell

    Refurbished homes are leased for steady rental income or marketed for sale at their full, renewed value.
